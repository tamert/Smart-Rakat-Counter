## en-US
Title: Rakat Counter
Short: Counts your rakats during prayer using the watch's motion sensor.
Full:
Rakat Counter keeps track of rakats for you while you pray, so you can focus on the prayer itself.

Wear the watch on your left wrist, tap START and pray as usual. The app follows your postures (standing, bowing, prostration, sitting) through the watch's gravity sensor. Each time you stand up again after prostration, it counts a new rakat and gives a short vibration. During the prayer the screen stays black with dim text so it doesn't distract you.

Long-press to finish. A tap does nothing during the session, so an accidental touch in prostration won't stop it.

- Works fully offline on the watch
- No account, no ads, no data collection
- English, Turkish, Arabic and Indonesian
- Tile for quick start

Designed for Galaxy Watch4 and newer (Wear OS 3+), worn on the left wrist.

## id
Title: Penghitung Rakaat
Short: Menghitung rakaat salat Anda dengan sensor gerak jam tangan.
Full:
Penghitung Rakaat mencatat jumlah rakaat saat Anda salat, sehingga Anda bisa fokus beribadah.

Pakai jam di pergelangan tangan kiri, ketuk MULAI, lalu salat seperti biasa. Aplikasi mengenali berdiri, rukuk, sujud, dan duduk melalui sensor gravitasi jam. Setiap kali Anda berdiri lagi setelah sujud, satu rakaat baru dihitung dan jam bergetar singkat. Selama salat layar tetap hitam dengan teks redup agar tidak mengganggu.

Tekan lama untuk selesai. Ketukan biasa tidak menghentikan hitungan, jadi sentuhan tak sengaja saat sujud aman.

- Berjalan sepenuhnya offline di jam
- Tanpa akun, tanpa iklan, tanpa pengumpulan data
- Bahasa Indonesia, Inggris, Turki, dan Arab

Dirancang untuk Galaxy Watch4 ke atas (Wear OS 3+), dipakai di tangan kiri.

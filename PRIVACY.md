# Privacy Policy

Rakat Counter (package `com.tamer.rekat`) does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The app reads the watch's gravity (motion) sensor only while a session is running, to detect postures and count rakats.
- Sensor values are processed in memory on the watch and discarded. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and app backup is disabled.
